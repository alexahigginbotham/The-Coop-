01 / CAMPAIGN OVERVIEW

02 / TARGET AUDIENCE

MADE FOR AMES.
MADE FOR EVERYONE.

Our campaign focuses on customers in Ames, Iowa, and the surrounding area, with two key audiences in mind: college students and local families.

COLLEGE STUDENTS

When classes, studying, or a night out leave students hungry, chicken fingers are an easy, familiar choice. The Coop offers a meal they can count on, with a delicious sauce to make it even better.

LOCAL FAMILIES

Families are always looking for meals that everyone can enjoy, especially when picky eaters are involved. Chicken fingers are a familiar favorite, and Coop Sauce gives families another reason to choose The Coop.

THE AMES COMMUNITY

By focusing advertising locally, we can reach customers who are close enough to turn interest into an actual visit or online order.
